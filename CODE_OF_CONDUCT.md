# Code of conduct

Patternwise is a place where people are learning — often while job-hunting and under stress. Keep it a place where
anyone can ask a "basic" question or open a first-ever pull request without feeling stupid.

**Do**

- Be patient and kind, especially with beginners and first-time contributors.
- Critique the work, not the person. Explain *why* when you ask for a change.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Credit other people's ideas and work.

**Don't**

- Harass, insult, or demean anyone — including about background, identity, experience level or language.
- Post others' private information.
- Submit content copied from paid courses, books or other sites and present it as your own.
- Spam issues or pull requests.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block contributors who
break these rules. To report a problem privately, email the maintainer at the address on their GitHub profile.
Reports are kept confidential.
